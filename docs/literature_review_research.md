# Literature Review — Research Log

## Objective

Identify academic literature supporting the
methods used in CustomerInsight AI.

## Research Topics

- RFM customer behaviour representation
- Unsupervised clustering
- Internal clustering evaluation
- Partition similarity and stability
- Explainable customer segmentation

## Research Positioning

CustomerInsight AI integrates established
methods rather than introducing a new
machine-learning algorithm.

The study evaluates customer segmentation
using multiple complementary perspectives:

1. Cluster separation
2. Assignment stability
3. Model sensitivity
4. Behavioural interpretability
5. Research evidence traceability

## Literature Review Limitations

The current selection mainly consists of
foundational methodological studies.

A broader systematic literature review has
not been performed.

Recent empirical studies and direct
comparisons with related customer
segmentation systems are still needed.

## Next Research Tasks

- Review recent customer segmentation studies.
- Compare their datasets and evaluation methods.
- Identify differences from this project.
- Verify all in-text citations and references.
- Complete reproducibility verification.
